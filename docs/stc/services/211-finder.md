## 2-1-1 Service Finder<


## Purpose

Can be used to identify a number of community services across BC which may support paramedic decision-making. 


Includes: 

Addiction 

Counselling 

Mental Health 

Victim Services 

Financial Assistance 

Food & Basic Goods 

Homelessness 

Housing 

Legal & Advocacy 

Transportation 

Indigenous Services 

Children & Family Services 

Disability Services 

Older Adult Services
