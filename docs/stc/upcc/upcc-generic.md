# Generic UPCC Referral Criteria

## Inclusion Criteria

• Age 17 – 70


• Vital signs within normal range (NEWS2 ≤ 4, no single score of 3)


• Not intoxicated by drugs or alcohol


• No acute mental health concerns


• Cognitively intact


• Able to ambulate safely & independently (inclusive of those with mobility aides)


• Patient can tolerate oral rehydration


• Willing to redirect to UPCC


• UPCC can accept intake

## Exclusion Criteria

• Serious trauma or dangerous mechanism of injury


• Cardiac symptoms


• Respiratory distress


• Abnormal vital signs (NEWS2 > 4, single score of 3)


• SIRS or possible sepsis


• Suspected stroke or TIA


• Recent seizure activity


• Acute mental health concern


• Acute substance use concern (withdrawal)


• Infectious disease requiring isolation


• Patients in Custody
